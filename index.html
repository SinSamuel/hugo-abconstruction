Works

Selected works from the field, hung as we would hang them. Each placard gives the address where the work stands. Select a frame to read about it.

Four trades, one crew.

Most jobs fail at the handoff between trades. We keep the whole sequence in house, from the first stud to the last coat of paint.

Framing

Structural and non-structural framing for tenant build-outs, new commercial shells and additions. We lay out from the plans, check every plane, and leave the next trade a straight wall.

  • Metal and wood stud partitions
  • Headers, blocking and backing for fixtures
  • Soffits, bulkheads and ceiling framing

Painting

Interior and exterior coating systems specified to the surface and the traffic it will see. Careful prep is most of the job; the finish coat shows it.

  • Spray, brush and roll application
  • Epoxy and high-performance coatings
  • Wall coverings and accent finishes

Decks

Commercial patios, rooftop terraces and restaurant decks, framed for load and drainage, finished in timber, composite or hardwood.

  • Structural framing and footings
  • Railings and guards to code
  • Stairs, ramps and benches

Detail finish

The quiet work: casing, base, crown, built-ins, reception desks and wall paneling. Tight joints, scribed fits and nail holes nobody can find.

  • Trim and millwork installation
  • Custom built-ins and counters
  • Final punch list and touch-up
